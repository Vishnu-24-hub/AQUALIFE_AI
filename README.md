## 2. Problem Statement (1M1B SDG Mapping)

> **Standard 1M1B Format**:
> *"How might we use AI to **detect fish diseases early, monitor water quality parameters, and optimize feeding schedules** so that **domestic aquarium keeping and aquatic pet care** can become more sustainable and biosecure?"*

### Problem Context & Real-World Need
1. **High Pet Mortality & Misdiagnosis**: Over 60% of domestic aquarium fish mortality is caused by preventable water quality degradation (ammonia toxicity) or misdiagnosed fungal/parasitic diseases (Ich, Fin Rot).
2. **Water & Feed Resource Waste**: Over-feeding uneaten food decays rapidly into toxic ammonia, forcing frequent, wasteful water changes and causing eutrophication when wastewater enters municipal drains.
3. **Ecological Invasive Species Hazard**: When pet fish grow too large or get sick, uninformed owners often release them into local rivers or ponds, leading to invasive species outbreaks (e.g. overgrown goldfish, plecos) that destroy native biodiversity (SDG 14).

## 3. Who is Affected by This Problem?

- **Aquarium Hobbyists & Pet Owners**: Experience fish loss, financial waste on incorrect medications, and tank failure.
- **Aquaculture & Ornamental Fish Farmers**: Suffer economic losses from undetected disease outbreaks in breeding tanks.
- **Local Aquatic Ecosystems**: Affected by invasive species release and nutrient pollution from untreated aquarium wastewater.

## 4. Role of AI & Technological Architecture

AquaLife AI integrates three core AI components to solve these challenges:

```
                  +-----------------------------------+
                  |         AquaLife AI Engine        |
                  +-----------------------------------+
                                    |
     +------------------------------+------------------------------+
     |                              |                              |
     v                              v                              v
[Multimodal Vision AI]     [Predictive Water Quality AI]     [RAG Knowledge Bot]
 - Fish Species ID          - Ammonia Toxicity Score          - SDG 14 Guidelines
 - Disease Pathogen Scanner - Nitrogen Cycle Simulation       - Invasive Species Prevention
 - Fin & Scale Diagnostics  - Preventive Water Actions       - Eco-Friendly Care Tips
```

1. **Multimodal Computer Vision**: Classifies fish species (Betta, Neon Tetra, Goldfish, Discus) and detects disease symptoms (Ich spots, Fin Rot fraying, Saprolegnia fungal growth) with confidence metrics.
2. **Predictive Water Quality Indexer**: Evaluates non-linear interactions between temperature, pH, ammonia (NH₃), nitrite (NO₂⁻), and nitrate (NO₃⁻) to calculate an overall Water Safety Index (0-100%) and trigger early toxicity warnings.
3. **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**: Answers user questions grounded on verified aquatic ecology guidelines, species compatibility matrices, and invasive species prevention facts.

## 5. Design Thinking Framework

### Stage 1: Empathize
- **User Needs**: Pet owners love their fish but lack veterinary expertise to spot subtle disease signs or calculate bio-load limits.
- **Pain Point**: Test strips give chemical numbers (pH, PPM) without clear actionable steps on how to fix toxicity safely.

### Stage 2: Define
- **Target User**: Domestic aquarium keepers, school biology labs, and small-scale ornamental fish breeders.
- **Current Gap**: Existing solutions are either generic web forums or complex expensive industrial aquaculture sensors.

### Stage 3: Ideate
- **Solution**: A unified, web-accessible AI platform combining instant photo diagnostics, interactive water safety sliders, smart bio-load feed calculations, and RAG advisory.

### Stage 4: Prototype
- **Working Web Application**: Fully interactive HTML5/CSS3/JS dashboard featuring real-time visual neural network scanning, dynamic gauge meters, chat assistant, and feed dosage calculator.

### Stage 5: Test & Refine
- Tested against common sample disease cases (Ich, Fin Rot, Fungal Infection, Healthy) and extreme water parameters (high ammonia > 0.5 ppm) to ensure early warning accuracy.

## 6. Responsible AI & Ethics Framework (Mandatory Compliance)

Every aspect of AquaLife AI adheres to the four core principles of Responsible AI:

| Principle | Implementation in AquaLife AI |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Fairness** | Models trained across diverse freshwater and marine species to avoid diagnostic bias toward common commercial breeds over native fish. |
| **Transparency** | Diagnostic outputs display exact confidence percentages and explain visual cues (e.g., scale lesions, fin fraying) rather than black-box decisions. |
| **Ethics** | Promotes eco-friendly treatments (aquarium salt, water changes, almond leaf tannins) before harsh chemicals. Strictly forbids advice encouraging wild poaching or invasive species release. |
| **Privacy** | Image uploads and water data process locally within the user session. No camera streams or personal identifiers are stored or shared. |

## 8. Working Prototype Screenshots & Demo Workflow

The prototype includes 5 interactive tabs:
1. **AI Disease Detector**: Drag-and-drop or select sample fish photos to receive instant diagnostic confidence, fin integrity score, and treatment steps.
2. **Water Quality Predictor**: Adjust sliders for temperature, pH, ammonia, nitrite, and nitrate to view real-time safety index % and nitrogen cycle balance.
3. **RAG Sustainable Assistant**: Chat interface for instant questions on tank cycling, live plants, and invasive species risks.
4. **Smart Feed Optimizer**: Enter tank volume and fish count to receive precise daily feed grams and prevent water pollution.
